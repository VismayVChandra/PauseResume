# Demo video script (target: 2–3 minutes)

**0:00–0:15 — Hook**
"Most resume tools make you fill out a long form from scratch. This one starts from your LinkedIn profile and the job you actually want." Show the landing screen.

**0:15–0:35 — Step 1: Target role**
Type a real job description (or a short title) into the target-role box. Say: "This gets captured first, so tailoring starts immediately — not as an afterthought."

**0:35–1:10 — Step 2: Import**
Show the LinkedIn "More → Save to PDF" export being uploaded (or click "Try demo profile" if uploading live is awkward on camera). Mention the paste-text and manual-entry fallbacks exist for anyone without a fresh export.

**1:10–1:40 — Step 3 & 4: Extraction + tailoring (AIService)**
While it loads: "Behind the scenes, one AI call extracts a neutral profile — your raw work history — and stores it separately. A second AI call tailors that profile toward the target role: it reorders experience, rewrites bullets, and pulls in matching skills. It's not allowed to invent anything — if the role wants something your profile doesn't have, it tells you instead of making it up."

**1:40–2:15 — Step 5: Review & edit**
Show the unified editable form. Point out: the AI disclaimer, the "gaps for this role" callout, and that hobbies/portfolio-link fields (which LinkedIn doesn't export) sit right alongside the AI-filled ones — everything is just editable. Make one live edit to a bullet point.

**2:15–2:45 — Step 6: Export**
Click "Download PDF," open it. Click "Download DOCX," open it in Word to show it's genuinely editable there too. "Same underlying data, two formats."

**2:45–3:00 — Close**
"From LinkedIn export to a tailored, downloadable resume in under two minutes." End on the exported PDF.
